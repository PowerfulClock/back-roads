# Back Roads

An open-world driving game in the browser: a small town and its country roads, live traffic, and a persistent police force that patrols, runs radar, makes traffic stops and chases you if you run. The cars, physics and engine sounds come from [Quarter Mile](https://powerfulclock.github.io/quarter-mile/).

**Play it at https://powerfulclock.github.io/back-roads/**

- 50 cars, including every muscle car from Quarter Mile (Camaro, Challenger, Charger, Mustang) with real recorded engine sounds
- Drive modes per car (Normal, Sport, Track, Drag Strip), turbo lag and boost gauge
- Built-in 0-60 timer (press G or the 0-60 button on the dash)
- **Online servers** (Game Mode → Servers): up to 4 players, pick a display name and drive together. Servers use default settings with medium traffic, 10 State and 10 City Police, and a 15-minute day / 15-minute night cycle. No account needed: players connect browser to browser
- Police with pursuits, PIT maneuvers, rolling and standing box-ins, spike strips, and LED light bars
- Day, sunset and night, traffic density and police settings in the menu

Controls: W/S accelerate and brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake, Q drive mode, X/Z gears, C camera, M map, G 0-60 timer. Controllers are supported.
